Umicom Platform Batch 18 — Suite and Inter-Application Runtime Foundation

- Adds Framework Umicom::integration contracts for application discovery,
  required/optional dependency resolution, presence, routing, envelopes,
  dispatch, shared sessions, suite launch planning, topology and health.
- Upgrades platform suite manifests to umicom.suite/2.
- Distinguishes required_members from optional_members.
- Enables Studio, Financial, Creator, Education and Complete suite planning.
- Adds native workspace suite list/show/plan/validate commands.
- Adds native workspace integration-graph command.
- Missing optional products no longer block a suite.
- Missing required products still block readiness.
- Keeps process launch/stop out of Batch 18 until executable conventions are
  standardised across product repositories.
